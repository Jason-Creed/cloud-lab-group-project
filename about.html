About Group 404

The story behind the directory, and the team who built it.

The Problem

Scattered vendors, no single view

Our campus has many food vendors, but they're spread out across different corners of campus with no central place listing what they sell, where they are, or what they charge. Students often end up sticking to the one or two spots they already know, missing out on cheaper or better options nearby.

The Solution

One page, every vendor

Campus Food Finder is a simple static website that lists campus food vendors in one place — name, food type, location, price range, and hours — so students can quickly compare options before deciding where to eat. It's built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and deployed with GitHub Pages.
